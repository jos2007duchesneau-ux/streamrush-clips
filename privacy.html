Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

StreamRush Clips is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own social accounts.

Data we access

With your authorization (TikTok scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish and video.list), the TikTok API provides your account's open ID, display name and username, the processing status of the videos you send through the tool, and the list of your own public videos with their view counts, which the owner uses to track the performance of each clip. We do not access followers, messages, contacts or any other personal data, and we do not post anything without the owner's action or prior approval.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only in the owner's macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and websites. Revoking access makes the stored tokens unusable; contact us to have any stored data deleted.
